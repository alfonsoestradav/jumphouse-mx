# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4, chosen because the user asked to deploy on Vercel and this is the native stack for that target.

## Users

Families, teens and groups of friends in Santa Catarina and the west side of Monterrey (Nuevo León) who need an indoor plan: a Saturday with kids, a birthday, or a weekday promo. Parents want price, hours, address and a WhatsApp path in Spanish. Jumpers want to see the park.

## Product Purpose

A marketing website for Jump House Trampoline Park so people can decide to visit or book a party without hunting through Facebook or Instagram. Success is a WhatsApp conversation or a trip to Plaza Santa Catarina.

## Positioning

The neighborhood trampoline park in Plaza Santa Catarina: unlimited jump time, weekday promos, birthday kid free, and only jumpers pay. Not a national chain with six locations.

## Operating Context

The business currently lives on Google Maps, Facebook (`JumpHouseMx`) and Instagram (`@jumphousemx`). There was no website. Contact is WhatsApp `81 3122 0641` and phone `81 3407 1953`. Hours published on social: 1:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spanish (`es-MX`) only.
- Content is taken from Maps, Facebook and Instagram. Prices and promos are as published on those channels and may change; the site says so.
- No online ticketing, waiver kiosk or multi-location selector.
- Primary conversion is WhatsApp.
- Do not invent attractions, reviews, or package contents that were not published. Attractions shown are those visible in their Facebook photos (trampolines, foam cubes, gladiator logs, basketball, climbing wall, parties).
- Photography on the site is from Jump House’s own Facebook/Instagram posts of the Santa Catarina park.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Jump House / Jump House Trampoline Park. Visual direction pinned by the user: same theme as Facebook and Instagram photos — dark indoor courts, magenta and lime neon, colorful pads, high energy, not a cream/editorial kids site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google Maps: Av. Lic. Manuel Ordóñez 322, Plaza Santa Catarina, 66350 Santa Catarina, N.L.; 13:00–21:00; coords 25.673675, -100.4579527.
- Facebook and Instagram: weekly promos, Jump Pack, birthday parties from $245/person, socks $40, unlimited time, all ages, only jumpers pay.
- Competitor sites used only for information architecture (pages a trampoline park website usually has), not for copying claims.

## Product Principles

1. A parent should know what it costs, when it is open, and how to get there in the first screen.
2. WhatsApp is the checkout.
3. Publish only facts from the business’s own channels.
4. The site should feel like the park at night, not like a chain template.
5. Every page ends with a way to go or to write.
